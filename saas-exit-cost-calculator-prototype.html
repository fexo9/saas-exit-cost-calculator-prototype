Assumptions, stated plainly: the fee rises once per year by the hike %; seats stay constant; no taxes. The one-time price is a number YOU enter — it is not a quote or a promise. What this does NOT model, and you must estimate separately: switching effort (data export, retraining, downtime), the risk that a one-time tool is abandoned or stops updating, and anything the subscription buys you beyond access (support, integrations, compliance). If the one-time route's migration + upkeep exceed the savings shown, the subscription is the honest winner — this calculator will tell you so.
